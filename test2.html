Before You Explore!

This map documents historic pioneer cemeteries and other graveyards in Franklin County, Indiana. Please read the following before visiting a site or using this information.

Access and Private Property

A cemetery's appearance on this map, no matter the rating assigned, does not give anyone the right to enter it. Many of these sites are on private land, those land owners control access. Mistakes can happen with labels, so always obtain the landowner's permission before entering.

Protecting and Respecting

Please do not disturb, move, remove, or damage any marker, stone, or other feature. Even if you mean well, Indiana law protects cemeteries and there are people trying to document and survey many sites. Please contact the DNR or a local representative if anything is amiss.

If you find human remains, or think you may have found human remains, leave them undisturbed and report them immediately to the County Coroner.

About This Information

This is a work in progress. Records come from ongoing and historical surveys both, and as such they may be incomplete or contain errors. Locations should always be taken as approximate and must not be used for legal, boundary, or property purposes. Contact information and correction information will be added when the site is published.

Before You Use Location

This feature shows your approximate position on the archive map for general reference only. It is not intended for:

  • Turn-by-turn navigation
  • Route planning or travel directions
  • Emergency dispatch or reporting your location to emergency services
  • Surveying, legal boundary determination, or property-line decisions

GPS accuracy varies with your device, weather, and tree cover, and cemetery and grave locations on this map are approximate. Many cemeteries are on private property or have no public access; please obtain permission and respect posted signs. Watch for uneven ground, sunken graves, and other hazards. In an emergency, call 911.
